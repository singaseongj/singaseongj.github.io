Privacy Policy

For Log For Everything App

1. Information We Collect

Although Log For Everything does not require registration, we and our third-party partners may collect the following data:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) or IDFA to personalize ads and measure ad performance.
  • Device Information: Model, OS version, screen resolution, locale settings, IP address.
  • Usage Analytics: App usage events, crashes, session duration via Firebase Analytics/AdMob SDK.
  • Approximate Location: Derived from IP address for ad relevance (no GPS/location permission requested).

We do not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS data, or other personal contacts information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data collected helps us to:

  • Deliver personalized ads and measure their performance.
  • Analyze app stability and usage patterns to improve features.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.

Advertising partners (e.g., AdMob) may use cookies and similar technologies. For details, see their privacy policies.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

We do not target or knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have collected your child’s data in violation of COPPA, please contact us to request deletion.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain analytics and advertising data for up to one year. Aggregated and de-identified reports may be kept indefinitely for internal analysis.

You can reset your Advertising ID from your device settings to opt out of personalized ads. To request deletion of any collected data, email us at fancy-maillot0o@icloud.com.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.2+) for data in transit. Third-party SDKs store data securely according to their policies. We continuously review security practices to protect your information.

6. Third‑Party Services

We integrate only the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (ads and analytics)
  • Firebase Analytics (usage tracking)

None of these services collect personal identifiers beyond what is listed above. For their full privacy practices, please review their policies.

7. Your Rights & Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access or request a copy of your data we hold.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of analytics or personalized ads by resetting your Advertising ID.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at fancy-maillot0o@icloud.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated in-app or through our website. The “Effective Date” below will reflect the latest revision.
