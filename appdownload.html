Manage Students — Teacher’s Guide & Download

Manage Students is a lightweight app that helps teachers keep rosters, quick notes, and attendance in one place. It’s built for speed—so you can record what matters during class without breaking flow—and for longevity, so your notes become a helpful record over time.

Who it’s for

  • Classroom teachers who need fast, reliable note-taking tied to student names
  • Club advisors tracking attendance and participation
  • Tutors who want a simple, portable way to maintain session notes

Key benefits

  • One-tap capture: note ideas before you forget them
  • CSV import/export: move data to spreadsheets or share with colleagues
  • Lightweight & responsive: works well on modest devices and spotty networks

Quick Start

  1. Install the app (see Download below).
  2. Create your first class: name it, set term or subject, choose color.
  3. Add students: add individually or import CSV (template provided).
  4. Daily use: tap a student to add quick notes; mark attendance; star important items.
  5. Export: at the end of week/month/term, export to CSV for backup or reporting.

Feature Deep-Dive

Rosters & sections: Organize multiple classes; filter by period or subject.

Quick notes: Each note is timestamped; keep them short and actionable (e.g., “Great progress on lab safety today”).

Attendance: Mark present/absent quickly; optionally add reasons or labels for interventions.

Search & tags: Find patterns by searching student names, tags, or keywords.

Exports: Export selected classes or time ranges. Use CSV to open in Excel/Sheets.

Shortcuts: Pin common actions (e.g., “Late arrival,” “Needs follow-up”) to reduce typing during class transitions.

Classroom Use Cases

  • Parent meetings: pull up recent notes to discuss progress with specific examples.
  • Interventions: track frequency of behaviors or supports over weeks.
  • Clubs: take attendance and activity notes without maintaining separate files.
  • Project-based learning: note milestones after each session to inform assessment.

Permissions & Data Safety

Local-first design: Core data lives on your device. If cloud features are used, they are limited to what’s necessary for sync/backup.

What’s collected: Only the minimum needed for features you enable.

Privacy in practice: Avoid sensitive identifiers when not needed; prefer initials or student IDs if your policy requires. Follow your school’s data rules.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

Can I import from Excel?

Yes. Export your sheet as CSV and use the import option in the app.

How do I restore after reinstall?

Keep a periodic CSV backup. After reinstall, import the CSV to restore classes and notes.

What if I teach multiple sections?

Create a class per section and use color-coding to avoid confusion.

How do I keep notes short?

Use tags or quick labels; you can expand details later when you export.

The app feels slow on my device.

Try closing background apps and ensuring you have free storage. Lightweight mode reduces animations.

I need to share with a co-teacher.

Export weekly to CSV and share via Drive/Email. (Co-editing depends on your workflow requirements.)
